# VoiceForge — 10-Minute Demo Call Script

For: clinic / home-services owner. Goal: a paid 14-day pilot by the end of
the call. Keep it conversational — no jargon, no "Vapi", no "webhooks".

## 0:00–1:00 — Open with permission

> "Thanks for taking the time. In the next 10 minutes I'll show you a phone
> assistant that answers the calls your front desk misses — and books the
> appointment while the caller is still on the line. Can I ask: roughly how
> many calls go to voicemail on a normal day?"

(Listen. Write down their number — you'll use it at minute 9.)

## 1:00–3:00 — Name the pain in their words

> "Most owners tell me the same thing: the phone rings while the tech is on
> a job or the receptionist is with a patient. 85% of callers who hit
> voicemail never call back. So every missed call is a customer who called
> your competitor next."

## 3:00–6:00 — Live booking demo

Run the assistant live (or play the recorded demo). Narrate:

> "Watch: a caller asks to book a tow. The assistant takes the name, the
> service, the day — and then reads the date back before confirming, so
> nobody ever shows up on the wrong day. The booking lands in your schedule
> and you get a text with the details."

## 6:00–7:30 — The photo step (the differentiator)

> "Here's what nothing else in this price range does. If a caller has a
> leaking pipe, the assistant asks them to text a photo while they talk —
> looks at it — and talks about what it sees. Your tech arrives already
> knowing what's wrong."

## 7:30–8:30 — The human escape hatch

> "And if a caller is confused or upset, it doesn't argue — it transfers
> them straight to your phone, and tells you what the call was about first.
> There's always an easy path to a human."

## 8:30–10:00 — Pilot terms and close

> "Here's how we start: a 14-day pilot on your main phone line. Fixed
> [₹25,000 / $399], half to start. If it doesn't answer and log every call
> in those 14 days, the deposit comes back — in writing, in the agreement
> I'll send today.
>
> The number we agreed at the start — [their missed calls/day] — at even
> half your average job value, the pilot pays for itself in the first week.
> Shall I send the one-page agreement today?"

## If they hesitate

- "I need to think about it" → "Of course. What would you need to see to
  feel comfortable? I can put the guarantee metric in the agreement first."
- "What if my customers hate talking to a robot?" → "It introduces itself
  as an AI assistant on every call, and any confused caller gets transferred
  to you with a briefing. Nothing is hidden."
- "Send me something to read" → send HANDOUT.md, then follow up in 2 days.
