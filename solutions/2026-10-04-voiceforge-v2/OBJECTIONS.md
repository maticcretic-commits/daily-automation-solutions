# VoiceForge — Top 5 Buyer Objections (with answers)

## 1. "Will it misunderstand my callers and embarrass me?"

"It introduces itself as an AI assistant on every call, so expectations
are set. It only books when it's sure — it reads the date back and asks
for confirmation before committing. And the moment a caller sounds
confused or asks for a person, it transfers them to your phone with a
briefing. Misunderstandings get a human, not an argument."

## 2. "What about my customers' privacy?"

"Callers are told the call may be recorded, right in the greeting. We
never store raw phone numbers — only an anonymous code. Photos are kept
30 days for the pilot and then deleted, and everything is wiped when the
pilot ends. That's written into the agreement, not just promised."

## 3. "What does each call actually cost me?"

"Nothing per call during the pilot — it's one fixed price for 14 days.
After the pilot, we agree a simple monthly price before anything
continues. No per-minute surprises, no usage bills you didn't approve."

## 4. "What if I don't like it?"

"Then you use the guarantee: if it doesn't answer and log every call in
14 days, your deposit comes back — in writing, in the agreement. And
after the pilot it's month-to-month; you can cancel with 7 days' notice."

## 5. "Who fixes it when something goes wrong?"

"Me, directly — you get my number, not a ticket queue. If the assistant
misbehaves on a live call, I tune it the same day. One tuning round in
week 2 is included in the pilot; after that, support terms are in the
agreement."

---

**Never say:** "It's AI, it learns on its own" (it doesn't — you tune it),
"it works 100% of the time" (nothing does — that's what the guarantee is
for), or anything about the underlying tech (Twilio, Vapi, webhooks).
Talk about answered calls and booked appointments, not infrastructure.
