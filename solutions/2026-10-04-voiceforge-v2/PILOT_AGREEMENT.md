# VoiceForge — 14-Day Pilot Agreement (template)

> Nitesh: fill every [BRACKET] before sending. Nothing here is legal
> advice — for larger deals, have a lawyer review.

**Between:** [Your business name] ("Provider") and [Client business name]
("Client"), dated [date].

## 1. Scope

Provider will run an AI receptionist on Client's phone line [phone
number] for 14 days, from [start date] to [end date]. The assistant
answers inbound calls, books appointments for [list of services], and
sends booking details to [Client contact + phone/email] by text.

## 2. Price and payment

Fixed pilot price: **[₹25,000 / $399]**. 50% ([₹12,500 / $199.50]) due
on signing; 50% due on day 14. Payment by [UPI / bank transfer / card].

## 3. Money-back metric (the guarantee)

> If the AI receptionist does not answer and log every call received on
> the pilot line during the 14 days, the advance deposit is refunded in
> full within 7 days.

"Answered" = the assistant picked up and spoke to the caller. "Logged" =
the call appears in the written call log. Provider delivers a
plain-language pilot report (calls received, calls answered, appointments
booked, transfers) on day 14 — this report is how both sides measure the
metric.

## 4. Support during the pilot

Client reports problems to [Nitesh's phone/WhatsApp]. Provider responds
within [e.g. 4 business hours] and fixes misbehavior the same day where
possible. One tuning round (greeting, services, booking rules) is
included in week 2.

## 5. Data and privacy

- Callers hear an AI-identity disclosure and recording notice on every call.
- Phone numbers are stored only as anonymous codes, never raw.
- Photos sent by callers are kept 30 days, then deleted.
- On pilot end or cancellation, all Client call data and media are
  deleted within 7 days; the pilot report (counts only, no personal data)
  is retained.

## 6. After the pilot

Continuation is month-to-month at [₹____ / $____ per month], cancellable
by either side with 7 days' written notice. The phone number remains the
Client's property at all times.

## 7. Limits

The assistant books only the services listed in §1, in [English/Hindi].
It does not give medical/legal/financial advice, take payments, or access
Client's other software unless separately agreed in writing.
